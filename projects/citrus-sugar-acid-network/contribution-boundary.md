# Contribution boundary

This page separates my independent work from collaborator-led components so that the portfolio does not imply ownership of the entire multi-omics framework.

## Independently completed by Chunyang He

- genotype-file preparation and transformation for eQTL analysis;
- principal-component analysis used as population-structure covariates;
- kinship-matrix construction;
- expression filtering and normalization for eQTL traits;
- mixed-model eQTL mapping;
- significance filtering and LD-based consolidation;
- cis/trans classification;
- genome-wide eQTL visualizations and interpretation;
- identification of 3,051 eGenes among 18,533 tested genes.

## Completed collaboratively

- integration of eQTL-supported genes with candidate sets generated by other analytical components;
- discussion and prioritization of four candidate regulatory hubs;
- design of promoter, allele, expression, and functional-validation strategies;
- interpretation of experimental evidence against network predictions.

## Currently being conducted by Chunyang He

- promoter-activity and downstream functional-validation experiments for selected candidates, in collaboration with the broader project team.

## Led primarily by collaborators

- WGCNA construction and module-trait analysis;
- random-forest feature-importance analysis;
- weighted multi-feature candidate ranking;
- differential-expression analysis;
- LASSO-based feature reduction;
- graphical-network construction and centrality analysis;
- assembly of the complete thesis-level workflow and most cross-method candidate intersections.

## Language used in applications

Appropriate:

- "led the eQTL component of a collaborative multi-omics framework";
- "participated in integrating eQTL evidence with collaborator-generated candidate sets";
- "helped prioritize four regulatory hubs and am conducting downstream validation";
- "genetically informed candidate regulatory network."

Avoid:

- "independently constructed the entire regulatory network";
- "developed every machine-learning and network component";
- "proved a fully causal gene regulatory network";
- presenting a single hub gene as the original scope of the project.
