# IRX10 regulatory genomics in citrus

**Status:** ongoing M.Sc. thesis research  
**Public-data status:** analysis summary only; unpublished data and figures are withheld

## Question

How does regulatory variation contribute to expression differences and fruit-quality traits across mandarin- and pummelo-derived citrus?

## Project origin

The work began as part of a broader citrus gene-regulatory-network project rather than as a single-gene study. Four candidates were prioritized from network and phenotype evidence. IRX10 became the leading candidate because it combined expression differentiation with biologically interpretable links to cell-wall formation and fruit-quality traits.

## My contribution

- independently performed the eQTL analysis;
- integrated eQTL results with collaborator-generated WGCNA evidence;
- compared promoter architecture across citrus lineages;
- evaluated expression patterns in relevant citrus groups and tissues;
- designed the analysis structure used to prioritize promoter and functional-validation experiments.

## Current interpretation

Comparative analyses indicate lineage-associated differences in IRX10 promoter structure and expression between mandarin- and pummelo-derived citrus. These results **suggest**, but do not yet prove, that regulatory divergence contributes to cell-wall and sugar-acid differences. Causal language is intentionally avoided until promoter-activity and functional assays are complete.

## Planned computational extensions

1. formal promoter-haplotype definition;
2. association between promoter haplotypes, expression, and fruit traits;
3. cross-environment stability of the expression-trait relationship;
4. integration with multi-year phenotype data;
5. reproducible release of sanitized code after manuscript planning and collaborator approval.
