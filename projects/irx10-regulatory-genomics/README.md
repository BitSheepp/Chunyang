# IRX10 case study within the citrus sugar-acid network

**Status:** ongoing collaborative M.Sc. research  
**Public-data status:** analysis summary only; unpublished data and figures are withheld

> This page is retained as a focused downstream case study. The main computational project is now documented at [Citrus sugar-acid regulatory network](../citrus-sugar-acid-network/).

## Why IRX10 is not the whole project

The work began as a network-level effort to identify regulators of soluble sugar and titratable acid accumulation in a citrus hybrid population. Co-expression, machine-learning, differential-expression, penalized-regression, eQTL, and network evidence were combined collaboratively to narrow thousands of expressed genes to a small set of testable regulatory hubs.

IRX10 was one representative hub selected for downstream allele, promoter, expression, and functional analysis. Its value is therefore not that the project began with a single known gene, but that a broader data-integration framework recovered a biologically interpretable candidate linking cell-wall xylan metabolism with fruit sugar-acid balance.

## Focused IRX10 question

How can inherited regulatory variation at a network-prioritized cell-wall gene contribute to expression and fruit-quality differences?

## Current interpretation

Comparative promoter, expression, and allele analyses support IRX10 as a useful test case for regulatory variation emerging from the network. The broader biological hypothesis is that cell-wall carbon allocation may interact with soluble sugar and organic-acid metabolism during fruit development.

The public portfolio intentionally avoids presenting the entire candidate network as causal. Network edges are treated as candidate relationships, while promoter and functional experiments provide gene-specific validation.
